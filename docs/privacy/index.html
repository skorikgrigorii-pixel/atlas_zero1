ATLAS ZERO — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026

ATLAS ZERO processes only the information required to operate its video production, publishing and platform-integration features.

1. Information processed

When a user connects a third-party account such as TikTok, ATLAS ZERO may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, profile information and publication-related data made available through the authorized API scopes.

2. Purpose

Information is used only to authenticate users, prepare content, publish authorized content, retrieve publication status and support related workflow functions.

3. Data sharing

ATLAS ZERO does not sell personal information. Data may be exchanged with connected platforms only as necessary to perform user-authorized operations.

4. Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed publicly.

5. Revoking access

Users may revoke third-party platform access through the settings provided by the respective platform.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as ATLAS ZERO and its integrations evolve.
